Executive summary

The PhytoBLOOM campaign used a Sequoia Scientific Hyper-a hyperspectral absorption meter (primary unit SN 8028) from August 2025. During the campaign the instrument went through three hardware configurations:

  1. Until 1 July 2025: full SN 8028 (original light/sensor body + original integrating sphere).
  2. 1 July to 24 September 2025: hybrid configuration after the original SN 8028 body stopped working. The team borrowed the light/sensor head from prototype unit SN 8019 but kept the original SN 8028 integrating sphere.
  3. From 24 September 2025 onward: restored full SN 8028 configuration.

Sequoia raised concerns about the head/sphere mismatch and recommended field calibration using the ND black spot (neutral-density filter plug paired with SN 8028). In September 2025 Sequoia delivered updated firmware (v1.11), a revised user manual, and MATLAB code including Hypera_RhoFromNDSpot. Harrison Smith implemented and applied this workflow.

Harrison performed a dedicated characterization run on 24 September 2025 (DI, black spot, SRFA, and post-restoration complete8028 files), then applied per-experiment ND-spot checks across PhytoBLOOM culture runs from September through November 2025. February 2026 meeting materials summarize rho drift across all strains and document remaining processing ambiguities.

ND-spot calibration method (manufacturer workflow)

Harrison Smith applied the procedure taught by Sequoia (MATLAB v1.2, 4 Aug 2025). The integrating sphere has a removable plug with a black ND spot of known absorption (cal.spotAbsorp in the factory calibration structure). Together with a Fluorilon white plug measurement of pure water, this recovers an updated sphere reflectivity spectrum rho.

Processing steps

  1. Collect pure water with white plug (whitePlug_purewater).
  2. Collect pure water with black ND spot plug (spot).
  3. Run Hypera_RhoFromNDSpot(cal, pureWater, T_pureWater, spot, T_spot).
  4. Replace cal.rho with the computed spectrum (Savitzky-Golay smoothed).
  5. Process sample and reference water with Hypera_Process to obtain absorption (1/m), water removed.

Standard daily file set (per experiment day)

From hypera_ndspot_proc.docx (Feb 2026 meeting):

  • 1 overnight DI
  • 1 flow-through DI
  • 1 0.2 µm filtered flow-through DI
  • 2 Hyper-a-only white plug measurements
  • 2 Hyper-a-only black spot measurements

Total: 7 Hyper-a files per experiment day.

Processing ambiguities still under review

  • Which pure-water file to use as main reference: 0.2 µm filtered DI vs white-plug-only?
  • Whether white-plug file must be the same as the main pure-water reference.
  • Whether to use start-of-run spot, end-of-run spot, or average after inspection.

Results and interpretation

ND-spot rho differs strongly from factory rho

Across strains, Harrison’s ND-spot-derived rho is lower than factory rho in the UV (≈0.94 at 300 nm vs ≈0.98 factory), crosses near 530–540 nm, and becomes higher in the red/NIR (up to ≈1.04–1.10 at 750 nm). A reproducible dip/feature near 670–680 nm appears in many spectra (likely chlorophyll-related structure in the sphere path during culture experiments).

Within-day stability improved over the campaign

  • September 16 EHUX (first ND-spot culture run): 1st and 2nd spot-derived rho diverge substantially, especially >700 nm (end-of-run rho >1.15 vs factory ~1.0). Suggests early protocol refinement or larger sphere drift on first application.
  • November runs (AAFF, CMAR, HCAR, GOCE, CLEP): start-of-run and end-of-run rho generally track closely, indicating within-day sphere stability once the routine was established.
  • 24 Sep SRFA check: absorption spectra with updated rho show expected CDOM-like shape (high UV absorption declining toward visible, near-zero by ~600 nm, slight negative values 700–750 nm in some scans). Multiple repeat curves cluster tightly.

Comparison with AC-9 / ACS (ongoing issue)

In December 2025 correspondence, Wonkook reported Hyper-a total absorption roughly half that of AC-9 or ACS for G. oceanica and M. polykrikoides, even after available preprocessing. Sequoia’s December meeting did not immediately attribute this to instrument fault; recalibration and Python re-implementation of the MATLAB ND-spot pipeline were pursued afterward.

Feb 2026 materials include 1114hcar_hya_acs.png and CMAR ac-s/Hyper-a slides for continued cross-instrument validation.
